Delete your data (keep your account)

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This page explains how to remove personal data from PersonaFolio without deleting your entire account.

How to delete or clear data in the app

  1. Open the PersonaFolio app and sign in.
  2. Open your resume / CV editor.
  3. Clear or replace the fields you want removed (for example name, email, phone, summary, experience, education, skills, projects, and other resume sections).
  4. Save your changes so the updated resume is stored.

What you can delete this way

  • Resume profile details you entered (contact info, summary, experience, education, skills, certifications, languages, projects, links, and similar fields)
  • Locally generated PDF exports you delete from your device

What remains if you keep your account

  • Your login account (email / authentication credentials)
  • Any resume fields you choose to keep
  • App preferences stored on your device (for example theme settings)

Request data deletion by email

If you need help removing specific data while keeping your account, email mb.farhan.m.1.9.9.8@gmail.com from the email address linked to your PersonaFolio account. Use the subject line: PersonaFolio data deletion request, and describe which data you want removed.
