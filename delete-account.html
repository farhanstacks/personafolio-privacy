Delete your account

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This page explains how to delete your PersonaFolio account and the data associated with it.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the PersonaFolio app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

What is deleted

  • Your authentication account (login credentials)
  • Your saved resume / CV data stored in our cloud database
  • Account-related metadata used for account status

What may remain

  • Short-term security backups may retain data briefly before permanent removal
  • Data we are legally required to keep, if any
  • PDF files you previously exported and saved outside the app remain under your control
  • Local app cache on your device until you clear app data or uninstall

Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the in-app flow, email mb.farhan.m.1.9.9.8@gmail.com from the email address linked to your PersonaFolio account and request account deletion. Include the subject line: PersonaFolio account deletion request.
