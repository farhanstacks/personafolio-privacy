## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to provide account access, resume building, AI assistance, and PDF export.

### 2.1 Account information

When you sign up or sign in, we collect:

- Email address  
- Password (stored and processed by our authentication provider; we do not store your password in plaintext)  
- Display / full name (if you provide it)  
- Account status metadata (for example, whether an account is deactivated and related timestamps)

Authentication and session management are handled by **Supabase Auth**. Session tokens may be stored locally on your device so you can stay signed in.

### 2.2 Resume and profile content you provide

You may enter resume-related information, which can include:

- Name, professional title, email, phone number  
- City and country (entered by you; we do **not** use GPS or device location services for this)  
- Professional summary  
- Work experience, education, skills, certifications, languages, projects  
- Profile / portfolio links  
- Template preference and builder progress  

This content is stored in our cloud database so you can access and edit it across sessions.

### 2.3 AI feature inputs

If you use AI-assisted features (for example, summary or experience suggestions), the relevant text you provide in the App may be sent to our backend and processed by a third-party AI provider (**Google Gemini**) to generate suggestions. Do not include information in AI prompts that you are not comfortable sharing with our service providers.

### 2.4 Device and local data

On your device, the App may store:

- Authentication session data  
- App preferences (for example, theme and onboarding state)  
- Generated PDF files you create or export  

We may request storage-related permissions on older Android versions to save or share exported files.

### 2.5 Communications

If you use password reset, email confirmation, or account reactivation flows, we (through our email delivery provider) process your email address to send those messages.

### 2.6 Information we do **not** collect

PersonaFolio does **not** currently:

- Sell personal information  
- Show third-party advertising  
- Use analytics trackers for advertising  
- Use crash-reporting SDKs beyond what the platform may provide by default  
- Access your contacts list  
- Access your camera or photo library for core resume features  
- Collect precise GPS location  
- Process payments inside the App

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account  
- Authenticate you and maintain your session  
- Store, display, and update your resume content  
- Generate AI suggestions when you request them  
- Export and share resume PDFs at your request  
- Send essential account emails (verification, password reset, reactivation)  
- Provide account deactivation and deletion features  
- Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues  
- Comply with legal obligations  

We do **not** use your resume content to train public advertising models, and we do not sell your data.

## 4. How we share information

We share information only as needed to operate the App:

| Recipient | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Supabase** | Authentication, database storage, and secure backend / edge functions |
| **Google Gemini** | Processing AI suggestion requests you initiate |
| **Email delivery provider** (via Supabase SMTP configuration) | Sending account-related emails |
| **Device share sheet / OS services** | When you choose to share or export a PDF |

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the App.

We do **not** sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 5. Data storage and security

- Resume and account data are stored with our cloud provider (Supabase).  
- Access to your resume data is tied to your authenticated account.  
- We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to our size and the nature of the data (encryption in transit via HTTPS/TLS, access controls, and secure authentication).  
- No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please use a strong, unique password and keep your device secure.

Local PDFs and cached preferences remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.

---

## 6. Data retention

- We retain account and resume data for as long as your account remains active.  
- If you deactivate your account, we may retain associated records as needed to honor reactivation or security requirements.  
- If you delete your account through the App’s account-deletion flow, we delete your resume data and authentication account from our systems, subject to short-term backups and any legally required retention.  
- Local device data is removed when you clear app data or uninstall the App (exported files you saved elsewhere may remain under your control).
